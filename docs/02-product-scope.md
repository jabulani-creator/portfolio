# 02 Product Scope

**Project:** The Website Guy — Digital Experience Consultant Platform  
**Stage:** Scope  
**Document type:** One-page scope boundary  
**Status:** v3 commercial release (Phases 0–9) plus **approved Phase 10 trajectory** in `docs/08-portfolio-studio-redesign-brief.md`, derived from `docs/01-Business_Diagnostic_OS.md` v3 (frozen)

---

## Project Goal

Build a premium, mobile-first digital platform for The Website Guy that makes the **productized Digital Experience Diagnostic** easy to understand and easy to book: SME owners and small institutional clients can quickly see this is not another freelance website pitch, understand exactly what the diagnostic includes, review real case-study proof, and book a scoped, priced first engagement — while the founder can publish and maintain offer copy, case studies, and proof without re-explaining the diagnostic on every sales call.

---

## In Scope For This Version

**Public website:** Home, Digital Experience Diagnostic, Case Studies, How I Work, About, and Start Here pages.

**Public clarity:** honest category headline, productized offer visibility, priced/scoped diagnostic explanation, proof of method, and a single site-wide CTA to book a Digital Experience Diagnostic.

**Positioning system:** replace builder-for-hire framing with **Digital Experience Consultant** framing across navigation, page titles, hero copy, and calls to action. Do not position as Business Systems Consultant or Business OS in this release.

**Digital Experience Diagnostic page:** sell the productized flagship offer with concrete deliverables, turnaround, and price — not a philosophy page or abstract method overview. Deliverables to present:

- Customer Journey Audit
- Website Audit
- Google Business Profile Audit
- Review Analysis
- Competitor Comparison
- Top Revenue Leaks
- Prioritized Action Plan
- 90-Minute Walkthrough Call

**Case Studies:** Observation → Evidence → Decision → Implementation → Outcome. Each case study must state what was built and what changed — not diagnosis alone.

**How I Work:** explain the diagnostic process briefly, including the honest byproduct line that broader business issues sometimes surface but are not the product being sold today. Surface build/implementation as a follow-on path, not the lead message.

**About:** communicate both halves of the advantage — investigation before build, and evidence-based implementation after diagnosis.

**Start Here / intake path:** one unambiguous conversion page for "Book a Digital Experience Diagnostic," with scope, deliverables, timeline, price, and next-step expectations stated plainly.

**Visual identity (v3 shipped):** calm, editorial, evidence-led design — strong typography, generous whitespace, minimal decoration.

**Visual identity (Phase 10 — see `08`):** same positioning tone, but **work-forward proof** (large case-study imagery, editorial case pages). Experience architecture reference: portfolio-studio model in `08` — not a literal clone of any reference site. Stripe/Linear/Notion remain **tone** references only; primary UX direction is `08`.

**Mobile-first experience:** fast scanning, low-friction reading, and clear CTAs for prospects arriving from referral, outreach, or search.

**Content management:** founder-editable content for case studies, offer copy, homepage copy, and site settings through the CMS.

**Proof publishing:** ability to publish at least one real diagnostic case study publicly — Kwisoko, with client permission and sensitive figures adjusted — as the first public proof asset.

**Copy iteration allowance:** homepage may test "Digital Experience Diagnostic" vs "Digital Experience Audit" in live copy without reopening the mother diagnostic document.

## Approved Next Build Increment: Ongoing Care / Retainer

The next scoped module is a standing **Ongoing Care / Retainer** offer surfaced as a visible product, not a buried post-project mention.

In scope for the first retainer increment:

**Retainer offer page or section:** monthly upkeep scope including content, SEO basics, and review monitoring.

**Repeat-engagement path:** make clear how diagnostic clients move into build/implementation and then into ongoing care.

**Cross-links from case studies and Start Here:** show the natural progression diagnostic → build → retainer without inventing billing or service-desk workflows.

**Founder-editable retainer copy:** scope summary, ideal client fit, and what ongoing care includes at a high level.

## Out Of Scope For This Version

Business Systems Consultant or Business OS positioning — that is Phase 3 on the positioning ladder and must not be sold in this release.

Founder Toolkit / framework library pages (Gate Framework, Advantage Index, Data Gravity, JTBD, Decoupling, etc.) unless explicitly re-scoped.

Full Business Diagnostic OS walkthrough as a public product page — the mother document guides internal thinking, not the public site architecture.

Full client portal, authenticated prospect accounts, proposal builders, and contract signing inside the site.

Automated booking, payments, invoicing, CRM, pipeline management, and sales automation.

Full marketing agency feature set: ad management, social scheduling, email campaigns, SEO dashboards, and analytics suites.

Public self-serve website quotes, theme pickers, page builders, and "get a site price" flows.

Multi-consultant team pages, hiring workflows, subcontractor management, and internal operations tooling.

Native mobile app, offline mode, advanced analytics, multi-country expansion, and franchise-style service packaging.

Automated WhatsApp, SMS, or email follow-up sequences from the website.

Full productised SaaS version of the Business Diagnostic OS for other consultants to license.

Blog/comments/community features unless explicitly added in a later content scope.

Diagnostic Library as a separate browsable module — not in v3; defer unless re-scoped.

Rebuild of every past portfolio project into full case studies in v1; only the approved first proof asset is required for launch.

Any build-delivery or client-workspace systems beyond what is needed to explain the offer on the marketing site.

Management consultancy framing, operational systems consulting, or broad business transformation language as the primary category.

## Priority Order

1. **Publish the productized offer:** turn the Digital Experience Diagnostic into a standalone, bookable page with deliverables, timeline, and price.

2. **Publish the first proof asset:** turn Kwisoko into the first public case study before or alongside the rebuild.

3. **Rebuild the public front door:** Home, category headline, Digital Experience Diagnostic page, Start Here, and site-wide CTA.

4. **Replace old information architecture:** remove Services / Portfolio / generic Contact framing in favour of Digital Experience Diagnostic / Case Studies / How I Work / Start Here.

5. **Ship the editorial visual system:** typography, spacing, tone, and layout that match evidence-led consultant positioning rather than agency decoration.

6. **Launch the core trust pages:** How I Work, About, and first Case Study.

7. **Stabilise founder publishing:** CMS-backed editing for case studies, offer copy, and homepage content.

8. **Build the approved retainer increment:** standing Ongoing Care offer and repeat-engagement path.

9. **Validate commercial gaps before deeper tooling:** final price, confirmed turnaround time, intake method, CRM, and proposal workflows.

10. **Defer client portal and delivery automation** until at least one paid diagnostic is sold using the new positioning.

## Constraints

This is a repositioning at the **right altitude** — Digital Experience Consultant, not Business Systems Consultant.

The site must make the productized diagnostic offer legible within seconds on mobile, including what the client receives and by when.

The project must remain operable by a solo founder without a large content or engineering team.

Sensitive client material in case studies must be permission-checked and anonymised where required.

The v3 rebuild favoured clarity and proof over decoration. **Phase 10 (`08`)** explicitly allows **evidence-rich, image-forward case presentation** and bounded micro-interactions—performance and credibility still override motion.

The flagship commercial action is **Book a Digital Experience Diagnostic**, not "Contact Me" or "Get a Quote."

Diagnostic price and turnaround are placeholders until real cost-to-deliver and market willingness-to-pay are confirmed — but the offer page structure must still be built now.

Homepage copy, headline language, and deliverable framing may iterate without reopening `01-Business_Diagnostic_OS.md`.

This is a repositioning and information-architecture project first; deeper operations software is later-phase work.

## Assumptions

Public users do not need accounts in this version.

The founder is the sole decision-maker, publisher, and sales intake owner.

The first public case study can be published once Kwisoko client permission and sensitive figures are handled.

Diagnostic pricing can be presented as a placeholder or scoped range on the offer page without building payments into v1.

Referral and direct outreach remain the primary acquisition channels during the first 90 days.

The existing Next.js + CMS foundation is sufficient for the marketing-site rebuild and content publishing in v1.

The productized offer page and Kwisoko case study are the two highest-leverage launch assets and should not wait for the full redesign to be finished.

The Ongoing Care / Retainer increment is approved next work, not a launch blocker.

This document is the scope veto: anything not listed in **In Scope** is later-phase work unless explicitly re-scoped.
